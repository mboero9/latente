Sports-data transparency

Sources and methodology

Latente gathers sports data through integrations with specialist providers and public sources where permitted. Our backend synchronizes information and presents it for consultation. We do not claim to provide official match timing for every game.

Results and scores

Results, match status and kickoff times come from the available sources for each competition. Some leagues are updated more frequently than others. Provider or synchronization delays may affect live updates.

Standings and top scorers

We publish tables, past results and statistics when supported by verifiable provider data. Temporary discrepancies may arise while federations approve fixtures, deduct points or correct recorded results.

Dates and time zones

The site allows you to check events in your selected time zone. An international game's sporting date may differ from its local calendar date.

Coverage and errors

Not all leagues provide lineups, scorers or match statistics. We prefer to mark incomplete information rather than present it as confirmed. Please report errors through the Latente issue tracker without sharing personal data.

Advertising transparency

When commercial advertisements are enabled, they will be labelled and separated from scores. Sponsors do not influence editorial coverage or sports statistics.
