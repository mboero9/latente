About Latente

Latente.cfd is an independent digital project bringing together sports scores, fixtures, standings and statistics for national and international competitions.

We aim for a fast, clear and accessible experience, with a particular focus on Argentine football. We cover other sports and international competitions whenever reliable data is available.

What you can find

Match results, tables, top scorers, fixtures, historical records and navigation between teams and leagues. Coverage depends on the competition and on the availability of each data provider.

How we work

We combine our own software with specialist sports-data providers. Refresh frequency depends on the sport, competition and each provider's quotas or restrictions. Read our sources and methodology.

Independence and rights

Latente is not an official website for any league, federation or club unless expressly stated. Sports trademarks and names belong to their respective owners. Scores may be delayed or corrected.

Corrections and suggestions

Report technical issues or suggest improvements using the public GitHub issue tracker. Do not share personal or confidential information there.
