Partnerships and advertising

Bring your brand closer to sports fans

Latente is an independent portal for sports results, fixtures, standings and club and national-team statistics. We are building an audience looking for reliable sports information, with emphasis on Argentine football, rugby and major international competitions.

Formats to discuss

  • Section sponsorship: clearly identified brand presence alongside a suitable sport or competition, subject to editorial compatibility.
  • Institutional banner: unobtrusive advertising placement that does not cover live scores or navigation.
  • Sponsored content: carefully reviewed and clearly labelled commercial collaborations, without changing sports data.
  • Affiliate partnerships: links to sporting goods when the publisher has an approved and trackable affiliate account, with proper disclosure.

What we can measure

Proposals rely on measured traffic and analytics rather than promised impressions or returns. We can arrange a limited pilot and report valid impressions, clicks and placement details afterward.

Rates are agreed individually according to audience, placement, duration and scope. This page neither collects payments nor guarantees results.

Get in touch

While we prepare a private business contact channel, you can open a public GitHub partnership proposal. Do not share personal details, confidential budgets or payment information in a public issue; we can agree on a private channel for discussions.

Editorial independence

Advertising and paid collaborations are always labelled. Sponsors cannot alter results, rankings, tables or statistical coverage. Gambling-related offers require separate regulatory, age and platform-policy review and are not accepted automatically.
